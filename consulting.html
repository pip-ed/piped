Research in review

My research sits at the intersection of industrial ecology, process engineering, and decarbonisation strategy. I focus on methods that make low-carbon transitions more measurable, comparable, and useful in practice.

That means working on cement and concrete systems, transparent life cycle inventory data, prospective assessment, and process safety questions that emerge as industry shifts toward lower-impact technologies.
