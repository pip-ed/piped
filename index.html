Research themes

01

Heavy industry decarbonisation

Applying chemical engineering and industrial ecology techniques to identify and reduce the environmental impact of heavy industry. My operational expereince spans oil and gas, specialty chemicals, and minerals, and my doctoral research focusses on cement and concrete decarbonisation through producing low-clinker cements.

Explore this theme →

02

Transparent and accessible life cycle data

Lack of inventory data is a major challenge in life cycle assessment. I'm working on improving access to transparent inventory data for a range of unit processes, including methods to better use and understand reported impact factors.

Explore this theme →

03

Process safety & the energy transition

The energy transition requiring legacy process systems to undergo rapid brownfields development with emerging technologies. My emerging research aims to understand and quantify the process safety risks of heavy industry decarbonisation, merging safety science with prospective system analysis.

Consulting

Academic expertise · Applied research

I'm available to provide independent research, analysis and advice.

  • Prospective & ex-ante LCA
  • Inventory data curation (e.g., site survey, process model, proxy selection)

  • Technoeconomic assessment

Analyses can be packaged for journal publications, company reports or as confidential in-house advice.
